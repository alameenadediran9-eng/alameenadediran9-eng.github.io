Zine
Machine

Turn notes into an eight-page mini zine you can print on one sheet and fold.

Separate pages with a line of three dashes (---). Start a line with # for a page heading. Six inside pages fit.

Style

Your zine, in reading order

Print sheet · landscape letter or A4

How to fold it

  1. Print the sheet landscape, at full size.
  2. Fold it in half the long way, then unfold. Fold it in half the short way, then in half again.
  3. Unfold to the half-sheet, and cut along the center crease from the folded edge to the middle.
  4. Open it up, fold the long way, and push the ends together so the cut opens into a cross. Flatten into a booklet.
